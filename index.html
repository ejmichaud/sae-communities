SAE latent communities

Groups of sparse autoencoder latents that tend to fire together, from the residual stream of Gemma-2-9B at layer 11. Pick a community to see how its latents are coupled and the documents they fire on.

How these communities were found

SAE. Gemma Scope residual-stream SAE, layer_11/width_16k/average_l0_118: 16,384 latents, about 118 active per token.

Couplings. We recorded which latents fire on each token across about 1 billion tokens of The Pile. From the covariance C of the binary firing indicators we estimate pairwise Ising couplings with the mean-field approximation, J = −C−1. A large |Jab| means latents a and b fire together (or avoid each other) more than the rest of their co-firing pattern explains.

Communities. We keep the 3 million strongest couplings as edges of a graph over all 16,384 latents and partition it with the Leiden algorithm (modularity, resolution ≈ 200, seed 2). This is the same partition as in the paper; its community #74 best matches the hand-picked latents that tile the character-count manifold. Only communities with at least 3 latents are listed.

Documents. For each latent we show the Pile documents on which it fires on the most tokens (first 1,024 tokens of each document, as the model saw them).
